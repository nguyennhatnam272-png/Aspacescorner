"A Space's Corner" is a research-oriented platform designed to connect Earth science and interplanetary exploration. We offer an organized collection of theoretical locations using geographical characteristics and environmental temperature, subsequently comparing them with extraterrestrial landscapes. By identifying terrestrial environments that resemble unorthodox conditions, we aim to provide valuable insights to aid subsequent Moon and Mars objectives. Our website also covers Earth geology, extreme conditions, and astrobiology in order to support researchers, students, and space enthusiasts in investigating potential analog sites. Our ambitions reach much further, though. Our team focuses on envisioning a brighter tomorrow for humanity and scientific breakthroughs beyond the cosmos. Earth may be the starting point, but it certainly isn't the end. 

MAIN FEATURES: 
🪐 Interactive celestial bodies 
📖 Explanatory dictionary 
🔖 Bilingual support
